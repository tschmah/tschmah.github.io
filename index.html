Research interests

Statistical machine learning, image registration, and geometric mechanics.

About

Prior to joining the University of Ottawa, I worked at Rotman Research Institute (Baycrest) and the University of Toronto, applying statistics and machine learning to medical images and other high-dimensional data sets, with Stephen Strother, Rich Zemel, and Geoffrey Hinton. From 2004 to 2007 I was a lecturer in the Department of Mathematics at Macquarie University, and before that at the University of Warwick (U.K.). I completed my doctorate in 2001 under the supervision of Tudor Ratiu at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne. Much of my earlier research was in geometric mechanics, where modern differential geometry is used to study classical mechanical problems.
